# Privacy Policy for Myntra Virtual Try-On Chrome Extension

**Last Updated:** [Current Date]

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Myntra Virtual Try-On Chrome Extension ("Extension," "we," "our," or "us") handles your information when you use our browser extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Gemini API Key**: Your Google Gemini API key is stored locally in your browser's Chrome sync storage. We do not have access to this key, and it is never transmitted to our servers.
- **User Photos**: When you upload a photo for virtual try-on, the image is temporarily processed and sent directly to Google's Gemini AI API for image generation.

### Technical Information
- **Extension Usage Data**: Basic usage statistics (such as feature usage) may be collected locally for extension functionality.
- **Browser Permissions**: The extension requires access to Myntra.com pages and Google APIs to function properly.

## How We Use Your Information

### Local Storage
- Your Gemini API key is stored securely in Chrome's sync storage and is only accessible by the extension on your device.
- No personal data is stored on our servers or transmitted to us.

### Image Processing
- **Photo Uploads**: When you upload a photo for virtual try-on, the image is sent directly to Google's Gemini AI API for processing.
- **No Local Storage**: Your uploaded photos are not stored locally or on our servers after processing.
- **Temporary Processing**: Images are only processed for the duration of the AI generation request.

### Extension Functionality
- **Myntra Integration**: The extension accesses Myntra product pages to extract product images for virtual try-on generation.
- **API Communication**: Direct communication with Google's Gemini API using your provided API key.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

### Google Gemini AI API
- **Image Transmission**: Your uploaded photos are transmitted to Google's Gemini AI API for image generation.
- **Google's Privacy Policy**: The processing of your images is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
- **No Additional Sharing**: We do not share your data with any other third parties.

### No Server Storage
- We do not operate any servers that store your personal information.
- All data processing occurs either locally in your browser or through Google's APIs.

## Data Security

### Local Security
- Your API key is stored using Chrome's secure storage mechanisms.
- All local data is protected by Chrome's built-in security features.

### Transmission Security
- All communications with Google's APIs use HTTPS encryption.
- We do not intercept or store any data transmitted to third-party services.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- **API Key Management**: You can view, modify, or remove your API key through the extension's options page.
- **Photo Control**: You choose which photos to upload for processing.
- **Extension Removal**: You can uninstall the extension at any time, which will remove all locally stored data.

### Data Retention
- **Local Data**: Your API key and settings are retained until you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data.
- **Uploaded Photos**: Photos are not retained after processing and are not stored locally or on our servers.

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the extension
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

## Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the extension's support channels.

## Legal Basis for Processing (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, our legal basis for processing your personal data is:
- **Consent**: You provide explicit consent when you use the extension and upload photos.
- **Legitimate Interest**: Processing is necessary for the extension's core functionality.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Say no to the sale of personal information
- Access your personal information
- Equal service and price

Since we do not sell personal information and only process data locally or through Google's APIs, most CCPA rights are not applicable to our minimal data collection.
